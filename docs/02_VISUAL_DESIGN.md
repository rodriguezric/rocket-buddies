# Visual Design

## Art Direction
Use a **clean, colorful 2D storybook/vector style** with chunky silhouettes, rounded geometry and expressive animation. The visual target is whimsical rather than technically realistic.

Space backgrounds may be dark, but playable objects should remain bright and readable.

## Shape Language
- Rocket Buddies: rounded, friendly forms.
- Meeps: soft blobs, bells and curves.
- Merfs: many legs but rounded and ridiculous rather than sharp/scary.
- Rocket: chunky toy-like construction.
- UI: large rounded panels, stickers, patches, stars and mission-card motifs.

## Meeps
Core silhouette:
- jellyfish-like upper body,
- exactly three tentacle-like legs,
- large expressive face,
- bouncy locomotion.

Individual Meeps are differentiated through body shape, markings, accessories, movement and personality.

Animation vocabulary:
- bounce/walk,
- excited wiggle,
- cheese smell reaction,
- eat cheese,
- scared hide,
- celebrate,
- sleep.

## Merfs
Core silhouette:
- large spider-like alien,
- twenty legs,
- readable central body/face,
- exaggerated cartoon motion.

Twenty legs do not need independent procedural simulation. Animate them in visual groups to preserve readability and production sanity.

Merfs should look troublesome, greedy and funny—not horrific.

## Rocket
The rocket is both vehicle and home. Exterior should be recognizable at tiny scale. Interior rooms should resemble a playful modular clubhouse in space.

Upgrades should visibly change the ship when possible: fuel pods, scanner dish, cargo modules, booster, Meep habitat windows.

## World Identity
Each destination gets a dominant visual motif:
- Moon — grey/blue terrain + bright cheese yellow accents.
- Mars — rusty terrain, giant vistas, dust.
- Mercury — harsh sunlight, long shadows, heat shimmer.
- Venus — layered golden clouds and atmospheric equipment.
- Asteroid Belt — silhouettes, moving rock fields.
- Jupiter system — giant striped planet dominating backgrounds.
- Saturn system — enormous rings cutting across the sky.
- Uranus system — cyan/teal palette and unusual orientation motifs.
- Neptune system — deep blues, wind/cloud motion.
- Pluto/Kuiper Belt — tiny sun, ice, quiet mystery.

## UI
UI should prioritize icon + short phrase.

Primary HUD may show:
- objective,
- fuel when relevant,
- Moon Cheese when relevant,
- contextual interaction prompt.

Avoid keeping every resource permanently visible.

## Feedback
Interactions should produce strong but gentle feedback:
- squash/stretch,
- stars,
- little sound stingers,
- Meep noises,
- playful text bursts,
- controller vibration where available.

## Camera
Exploration: stable 2D camera with mild smoothing.
Flight: camera leads slightly in travel direction.
Mischief encounters: framed like a playful confrontation.
Avoid excessive screen shake.
