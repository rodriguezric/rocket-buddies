# Game Mechanics

## Resources

### Fuel
Fuel constrains travel and creates simple planning.
- Destinations have travel costs.
- Missions should never unknowingly strand a young player.
- Before launch, navigation clearly shows required round-trip fuel.
- Emergency recovery exists if the player makes a mistake.

Upgrades increase capacity/efficiency and unlock farther destinations.

### Moon Cheese
Moon Cheese is a collectible, mission resource and comedy device.
Uses can include:
- feeding/luring Meeps,
- distracting Merfs,
- mission objectives,
- certain gadgets,
- optional collection goals.

Do not turn it into an oppressive economy.

### Stars
Stars are earned primarily from adventures and discoveries. Use them for upgrades/cosmetics/unlocks.

## Exploration
Basic verbs:
- move,
- interact,
- scan,
- inspect,
- collect,
- use contextual gadget.

Exploration maps are compact and dense rather than large empty worlds.

## Scanning
Scanner reveals points of interest:
- Meep signals,
- cheese deposits,
- discoveries,
- mission clues,
- unusual objects.

Scanning creates a pulse and directional/readability feedback rather than merely opening a text menu.

## Discoveries
A discovery is an interactable piece of real-world space knowledge.

Sequence:
1. Notice something interesting.
2. Scan/inspect/use it.
3. Receive a short fact in context.
4. Add it to the Space Journal.
5. Optionally use that knowledge later.

## Meep Rescue
Rescue is an objective category, not one fixed mechanic. A Meep may be:
- trapped,
- lost,
- hungry,
- hiding,
- stranded across a hazard,
- chased by a Merf.

Cheese can often help but should not solve every rescue.

## Merf Mischief
Merf encounters use non-threatening conflict.

Possible verbs:
- distract,
- dodge,
- chase,
- puzzle,
- use gadget,
- offer cheese,
- defend cheese,
- cooperate,
- turn-based silly ability exchange.

Violence is abstract/cartoonish. The design goal is **outsmart**, not destroy.

## Rocket Flight
Arcade-lite controls:
- steer,
- accelerate/boost as appropriate,
- collect,
- avoid.

Flight duration is short. Distance changes encounter complexity, not real-time travel duration.

## Cheese Hunting
A scanner/digging activity:
- hidden deposits,
- limited or scored scans,
- spatial clues,
- rare cheese varieties.

Failure still yields something useful whenever possible.

## Environmental Mechanics
Destination properties become mechanics:
- lower gravity changes jumps,
- dust obscures clues,
- sunlight powers equipment,
- temperature requires route/equipment planning,
- wind affects probes,
- orbital environments affect flight.

## Buddy System
Initial single-player implementation may have one directly controlled Buddy with the second following/supporting.

Later mechanics:
- buddy switches,
- contextual assist,
- simultaneous cooperative actions,
- local co-op.

Systems must not assume only one Player node exists.

## Difficulty
Prefer optional assistance rather than conventional difficulty labels:
- stronger hints,
- longer timers,
- simplified hazards,
- navigation assistance.

## Saving
Persist after:
- mission completion,
- return to rocket,
- purchases/upgrades,
- discoveries,
- Meep rescues.

Autosave should be obvious and reliable.
